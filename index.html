Welcome aboard

Your next great watch starts here

AV Movies brings movies, series, anime and live television into one focused experience. Here is a quick tour before you start exploring.

This guide takes less than a minute and can be replayed anytime from the help button or Settings.
Step 1 · Discover

Find exactly what you feel like watching

Use the tabs to move between Home, Live TV, Movies, Series and My List. Search opens the Vibe Explorer and finds titles or live channels from anywhere in the app.

Select any poster to see its rating, story, trailer and episodes before you press play.
Step 2 · Watch

Open a title and press Play now

Choose a movie card, review its details, then select Play now. Series include a season and episode guide so you can jump straight to the right episode.

Use My List to save titles. Anything you start watching is also added to Continue Watching automatically.
Step 3 · Change players

Switch playback source whenever you need

Inside the movie player, use the Playback source menu directly below the video. You can also set your preferred source in Settings → Playback source.

If one source is unavailable, try another. AV Player 2 supports movies only; AV Player and ScreenScape can support series.
